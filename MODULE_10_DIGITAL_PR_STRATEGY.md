## JOURNALIST OUTREACH STRATEGY

### HARO (Help A Reporter Out) Strategy

**Platform:** HARO.com  
**Frequency:** 3 queries/day (respond to relevant ones only)  
**Annual Goal:** 10-15 journalist placements

#### HARO Response Strategy
1. **Join HARO** (free daily emails)
2. **Set filters** for industry/topics
3. **Respond within 2 hours** with expert insights
4. **Provide quotes** with company context
5. **Include company name & link** naturally

#### Preferred HARO Topics
- Web development trends
- AI in enterprise
- Business automation
- Digital transformation
- Software development best practices
- Tech leadership
- Remote work in tech
- Pakistan tech industry

#### Sample HARO Pitches

**Topic: "AI's Impact on Business"**
```
Nexora Studio has implemented 50+ AI solutions in enterprise 
environments. Our research shows AI implementation ROI averages 
300% in first year. Key challenge: finding skilled AI engineers. 
Our team can provide market insights and implementation expertise.

Contact: hello@nexorastudio.com
```

## CRISIS COMMUNICATION PLAN

### Crisis Response Protocol

1. **Immediate (Day 1):**
   - Assess issue severity
   - Prepare factual statement
   - Notify leadership
   - Prepare response

2. **Response (Days 1-2):**
   - Issue official statement
   - Reach out to affected parties
   - Provide transparent information
   - Correct misinformation

3. **Follow-up (Days 3+):**
   - Regular updates
   - Demonstrate corrective actions
   - Rebuild trust
   - Return to normal operations

### Key Messages
- **Quality:** "We maintain highest standards"
- **Reliability:** "We stand behind our work"
- **Transparency:** "We communicate openly"
- **Customer-first:** "Client satisfaction is priority"
