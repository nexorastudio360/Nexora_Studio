## TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE

### Low Blog Traffic?
1. Verify SEO optimization (keywords, meta tags)
2. Check internal linking structure
3. Promote on social media
4. Share with newsletter subscribers
5. Reach out to similar content authors for linking

### Slow Backlink Growth?
1. Increase guest post outreach (target 2-3/week)
2. Activate broken link building strategy
3. Follow up on resource page opportunities
4. Leverage PR coverage for backlinks
5. Review backlink quality (avoid spammy sources)

### Low Profile Referral Traffic?
1. Ensure profiles are complete and optimized
2. Add CTA/link to profiles
3. Verify profiles are indexable
4. Cross-promote profiles on social
5. Include profile links in email signature

### Inconsistent Brand Mentions?
1. Audit all external mentions (Google Alerts)
2. Contact websites with inconsistent info
3. Request corrections to NAP
4. Update internal systems with correct info
5. Create brand guidelines document
