## PART 3: AI SEARCH OPTIMIZATION

### Google AI Overview Optimization

**Strategy:** Create authoritative content that AI summaries will cite

- **Clear headers** for information extraction
- **Definitional content** for snippets
- **Data/statistics** for citations
- **Multiple perspectives** on topics
- **Expert quotes** for authority
- **Use cases** for practical examples

### ChatGPT Search Optimization

**Strategy:** Content that ChatGPT users find most valuable

- **Actionable advice** over theory
- **Step-by-step guides** for implementation
- **Real examples** from actual companies
- **Code samples** for technical content
- **Before/after** case studies
- **Links to original sources** (shows integrity)

### Perplexity AI Optimization

**Strategy:** Structured content for research purposes

- **Clear definitions** at beginning
- **Properly sourced** information
- **Multiple viewpoints** on controversial topics
- **Citations** throughout
- **Fact-based** rather than opinion
- **External links** to original research

### General LLM Optimization

**Key Principles:**
1. **Entity clarity** - Clear who/what you're discussing
2. **Semantic relevance** - Context around topics
3. **Source attribution** - Give credit to sources
4. **Structured data** - Use JSON-LD for clarity
5. **Comprehensive coverage** - Answer follow-up questions
6. **Original research** - Data and insights
